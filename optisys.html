optiSYS

Keeps your PC responsive and your connection smooth — quietly in the background.

One switch. Nothing to configure.

01 Memory

Frees cached memory when system RAM pressure rises, keeping your computer responsive under heavy load.

02 Battery

Reduces background polling when unplugged to extend battery runtime without altering your Windows power settings.

03 Wi-Fi

Stabilizes network latency by suppressing background Wi-Fi scanning during active connections.
