optiSYS

Keeps your PC responsive and your connection steady in the background.

What it does

  • MemoryIt frees cached memory when RAM gets tight, so the machine stays usable under load.
  • BatteryIt polls less when you are unplugged and leaves your Windows power settings alone.
  • Wi-FiIt stops background Wi-Fi scanning while you are connected, which keeps latency steady.
