optiSYS

    


    

      
Keeps your PC responsive and your connection steady in the background.

      
Download for Windows

    


    


    

      
What it does

      
Memory It frees cached memory when RAM gets tight, so the machine stays usable under load.

      
Battery It polls less when you are unplugged and leaves your Windows power settings alone.

      
Wi-Fi It stops background Wi-Fi scanning while you are connected, which keeps latency steady.
