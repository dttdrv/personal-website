What it does

      
On-device agent It reads and edits files in a folder on your phone and runs git there. It talks to the model directly from the phone.

      
Model choice It works with OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Google, xAI, DeepSeek, Mistral or your own endpoint, and your keys stay in the Android Keystore.

      
Workspaces Every task gets its own git repo. You can branch, read the diff and commit from the chat.

      
Private It collects nothing and does not need an account. Your files stay in Android scoped storage on the phone.
