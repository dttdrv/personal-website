PhoneCode

An AI coding agent that runs entirely on your Android phone.

Your code, your keys, your phone.

01 On-device agent PhoneCode reads, writes, and edits files in project workspaces, runs git, searches the web, and calls the model provider you choose.
02 Bring your own provider Pick from OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Google, xAI, Groq, DeepSeek, Mistral, Together, or sign in with ChatGPT. API keys live in the Android Keystore.
03 Projects and chats Each chat is its own project folder and git repository. Switch branches, commit snapshots, and push or pull from the chat.
04 Privacy by construction No backend, no telemetry, and no account. Crash logs stay local, export and import use a file you choose, and Auto Backup skips your secrets.
