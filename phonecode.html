PhoneCode

A coding agent that runs on your Android phone.

What it does

  • On-device agentIt reads and edits files in a folder on your phone and runs git there. It talks to the model directly from the phone.
  • Model choiceIt works with OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Google, xAI, DeepSeek, Mistral or your own endpoint, and your keys stay in the Android Keystore.
  • WorkspacesEvery task gets its own git repo. You can branch, read the diff and commit from the chat.
  • PrivateIt collects nothing and does not need an account. Your files stay in Android scoped storage on the phone.
