PhoneCode

An AI coding agent that runs entirely on your Android device.

Your code, your keys, your phone.

01 On-device agent

PhoneCode reads, writes, and edits files in local workspaces, runs git commands, and interacts directly with language models without intermediary proxy servers.

02 Model provider choice

Connect to OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Google, xAI, DeepSeek, Mistral, or custom endpoints. API keys are stored securely in the hardware-backed Android Keystore.

03 Isolated workspaces

Each task runs in its own git repository folder. Create branches, inspect diffs, and commit snapshots directly from the chat interface.

04 Private by design

No analytics, no telemetry, and no account requirements. Your workspace files remain strictly within Android scoped storage on your device.
